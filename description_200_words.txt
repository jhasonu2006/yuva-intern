This capstone project builds an end-to-end machine learning solution to predict customer churn for a telecom company, TeleConnect India. I first defined the business problem, objectives, scope and success metrics, then prepared a dataset of 7,000 customers by removing duplicates, filling missing values and engineering new features such as average monthly spend, number of add-ons, new-customer flag and high support calls. Exploratory analysis showed that month-to-month contracts, first-year customers, fibre internet and repeated support calls have the highest churn. I compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting using 5-fold cross-validation and selected Logistic Regression with a test ROC-AUC of about 0.83. Instead of relying on accuracy, I tuned the decision threshold to maximise business net benefit and showed that targeting high-risk customers is profitable, while sending offers to everyone causes a loss. I also analysed feature importance, errors, limitations and future improvements such as real data, time-based validation, SHAP and uplift modelling. The report ends with practical recommendations: contract upgrade offers, a first-year onboarding programme, fibre service review, support escalation and a monthly churn dashboard. The attached Python code, dataset and charts make all results fully reproducible, and the report documents the problem statement, methodology, data preparation, model results, visualizations, insights, limitations and future scope in a clear structure for reviewers.
